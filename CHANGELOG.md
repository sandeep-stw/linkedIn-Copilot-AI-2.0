# Changelog

## 0.1.0 — 2026-10-02
First student release. Ships as a **Student Kit** (unzip → open folder → `/linkedin-copilot`) and as a Claude Code plugin.
- One command, `/linkedin-copilot`, sets you up and runs your daily session.
- Setup reads your Upwork profile, résumé PDF and/or your own LinkedIn profile, then offers 3 ranked directions to choose from.
- Daily, weekly and monthly routines that prepare drafts and tasks automatically (`li-daily`, `li-weekly`, `li-monthly`).
- Browser-assisted LinkedIn workflow: Claude opens the right page, you paste and click. Nothing is sent or posted for you.
- Settings in plain Markdown (`linkedin-copilot/settings/PREFERENCES.md`); dashboard at `linkedin-copilot/dashboard/index.html`.
