# Changelog

## 0.2.0 — 2026-10-02
Find jobs and win clients, not just network.
- New `li-job-search` (career mode): daily LinkedIn Jobs search links, paste a post to get a 0–100 fit score
  and gaps, tailored résumé bullets and cover note, referral contacts, application tracking, follow-up
  reminders and interview prep. LinkedIn job posts only; you open the posts and you apply.
- New `li-sales-pitch` (business mode): pitch kit (value statement, 30-second pitch, one-page offer,
  price options, objection answers) and a deal pipeline from conversation to discovery call, proposal and close.
- Daily and weekly runs include the new steps; dashboard has Jobs and Sales views.
- New settings: job date posted, workplace, experience level, applications per week, minimum fit, follow-up timing.
- Student guide: new Step 4 "Find jobs or win clients".

## 0.1.0 — 2026-10-02
First student release. Ships as a **Student Kit** (unzip → open folder → `/linkedin-copilot`) and as a Claude Code plugin.
- One command, `/linkedin-copilot`, sets you up and runs your daily session.
- Setup reads your Upwork profile, résumé PDF and/or your own LinkedIn profile, then offers 3 ranked directions to choose from.
- Daily, weekly and monthly routines that prepare drafts and tasks automatically (`li-daily`, `li-weekly`, `li-monthly`).
- Browser-assisted LinkedIn workflow: Claude opens the right page, you paste and click. Nothing is sent or posted for you.
- Settings in plain Markdown (`linkedin-copilot/settings/PREFERENCES.md`); dashboard at `linkedin-copilot/dashboard/index.html`.
