Your LinkedIn Copilot, step by step

Follow the seven steps in order. Claude researches, drafts and keeps track; you read, paste and click in LinkedIn.

Setup 10 minDaily about 15 minNeeds Python 3.9+
Step 1 of 7

Install and open

  1. Unzip and move the kit folder

    Put it somewhere permanent, such as Documents. This folder becomes your personal campaign: your settings and progress are saved inside it.

  2. Open the folder in Claude

    Claude desktop app → Code tab → choose the kit folder. Using the terminal instead? Open it in the folder and run claude.

  3. Type the start command

    /linkedin-copilot

Python missing? Install it from python.org and tick Add to PATH during setup, then restart Claude. Check with python --version.

Step 2 of 7

Set up your campaign

  1. Claudeasks which sources you have. Give as many as you can; more sources mean better options.

    SourceHow you share it
    Upwork profileClaude reads it through the Upwork connector. Read only, nothing changes on Upwork.
    Résumé PDFGive the file path, for example C:\Users\...\Documents\Resume.pdf
    LinkedIn profileEasiest: on your profile choose Resources → Save to PDF and give that file. Or sign in yourself in Claude's browser pane and Claude reads your own profile once.

  2. Clauderesearches and shows 3 ranked options (roles, or services if you sell), each with evidence, fit, gaps and confidence.

  3. Youpick one. You can also ask to compare them, see the evidence, or research another direction.

  4. Claudesets up your campaign: profile improvements, the first post drafts and a first list of about 30 people.

  5. Yousay yes to the daily, weekly and monthly schedule. Then open Scheduled in the sidebar and click Run now once on each task, so later runs don't stop to ask for permission.

Never type a password into the chat. If you use the browser pane, you sign in yourself. Claude never sees or stores passwords.

Step 3 of 7

Work your daily tasks

At 08:30 on working days the copilot prepares drafts for you while the Claude app is open. Then each task follows the same loop:

ClaudePrepares

Shows what to do, why, and a ready draft.

ClaudeOpens

Opens the one LinkedIn page this task needs.

YouAct

Read, paste, adjust and click Connect, Send or Post.

YouConfirm

Type done, or skip if it doesn't feel right.

Example task
P2

Connection note to a platform engineering lead

~3 min · prepared

Why: leads the team behind the role you chose and posted about hiring last week.

Hi Asha, I read your post about scaling your platform team. I've spent four years building internal developer platforms and would value following your work. Thanks!

After sending in LinkedIn, type done. Claude records it and moves to the next task.

Only have a few minutes? Say "I have 5 minutes" and you get the single most valuable task.

Step 4 of 7

Find jobs or win clients

Your goal decides which track you follow. Career mode finds and wins jobs from LinkedIn job posts. Business mode turns conversations into paying clients. Claude sets the mode from the option you picked in Step 2.

Career mode: land a job

  1. Claudeprepares LinkedIn Jobs search links each day, filtered to your role, location and recent posts.

  2. Youopen the links and paste 2 to 5 posts that look right (the link plus the job description).

  3. Claudescores each post from 0 to 100 against your real experience and names the gaps.

  4. Claudetailors your résumé bullets and a short cover note, and finds people at that company who could refer you.

  5. Youapply on LinkedIn or the company site, then type applied.

  6. Claudedrafts a follow-up after 7 working days and prepares you when an interview comes.

Business mode: win clients

  1. Claudebuilds your pitch kit: one-line value statement, 30-second pitch, one-page offer, price options and answers to common objections.

  2. Youapprove it. Price options stay labelled as guesses until someone pays.

  3. Claudespots real needs in your conversations and opens a deal with a dated next step.

  4. Claudedrafts each step: a discovery call invite and questions, then a proposal in the buyer's own words.

  5. Yousend it and type sent. Claude follows up and helps with objections.

  6. Youtell Claude when a deal is won or lost.

No cold pitching: Claude never pitches in a first message. Selling starts after a conversation shows a real need, which keeps your messages welcome and your account safe.

Step 5 of 7

Handle replies and stay in touch

  1. Youtype "I got a reply" and paste the message.

  2. Claudepauses follow-ups for that person and drafts your answer.

If someone says no or asks you to stop, paste it too. Claude marks them do not contact and cancels every planned message to them, permanently. Only you can undo that.

No reply? Claude suggests a first follow-up after 5 working days and a second after 7 more. After that the person moves to a slow, long-term list instead of being forgotten.

Staying in touch

Every person you're building a relationship with has a warmth and a next touch date. Claude keeps both up to date from what you report.

WarmthWhoTouch every
HotReplying, a meeting planned, or an open opportunity4 working days
WarmFriendly, replies now and then10 working days
ColdNo response yet, or "not now"25 working days

  1. Claudedrafts one useful touch when it's due: a comment on their post, congratulations on real news, a helpful article, or a reply to something they asked. Never an empty "just checking in".

  2. Yousend it and type done. The next touch date updates by itself.

  3. Youpaste "not now" replies. Claude saves their reason and brings them back on the date they suggested.

  4. Claudewarns you each week about who is going cold and keeps every person's status and its history accurate.

Keep your LinkedIn account safe

LinkedIn bans bots and automation that send, connect, post or collect data, even inside your own browser. The copilot is built so that you do every action on LinkedIn. That is what protects your account.

Do

  • Read every draft and adjust it to your voice.
  • Confirm only things that are true about you.
  • Send a few thoughtful messages a day.
  • Tell Claude when someone says no.

Don't

  • Ask Claude to click Send, Connect or Post for you.
  • Let a draft claim results you didn't achieve.
  • Mass-send or install LinkedIn automation extensions.
  • Contact people again after they declined.

FAQ and fixes

"python is not recognized"

Install Python from python.org, tick Add to PATH, then restart Claude.

The 08:30 preparation didn't run

Scheduled runs need the Claude app open. A missed run happens the next time you open it.

Upwork import failed

Add the Upwork connector in Claude, or paste your Upwork profile text into the chat instead.

Something looks wrong in my data

Say "check my copilot data". Backups are kept automatically in linkedin-copilot/data/.backups/.

How do I update to a new kit version?

Unzip the new kit anywhere. Then, in your current campaign folder, tell Claude "Update my copilot from" followed by the new kit's folder path. Your settings and data are kept.

Is my data shared?

No. Everything stays on your computer in the linkedin-copilot folder. No passwords or cookies are stored. Don't share your data folder.
